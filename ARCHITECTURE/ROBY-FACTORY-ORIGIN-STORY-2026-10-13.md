## The third trajectory — ViP Clinic Complete

The present **ViP Clinic Complete** project is a distinct engineering trajectory: preserve the historical clinic workflow while developing a governed, independently verifiable EMR foundation. It is not evidence that either earlier UI version is production-ready.

As of this record, the owner and assistant are examining Stage 7's isolated PostgreSQL foundation. The **C1-N V2** synthetic transaction demonstrated rejection of a missing completion-event reference by the intended foreign key, followed by `ROLLBACK`. **C1-O** inspected the operation-receipt schema without database writes. These results are bounded structural evidence, **not** proof of authenticated clinical runtime, live receipt replay, clinical release, or production acceptance.

## What the experiences taught us

**Vibe coding is an acceleration method, not a transfer of authority.** A reusable nucleus can shorten the path from established knowledge to a visual prototype; building from scratch can serve a different design goal. Neither approach removes the need for bounded scope, contracts, testing, provenance, and explicit owner decisions.

A mnemonic for this reflection — **not a replacement constitution**:

> VIBE GENERATES.  
> NUCLEUS GUIDES.  
> CONTRACTS BOUND.  
> PROOF DECIDES.

The owner's collaborative triangle is **the owner (idea and decision), the assistant (planning and critical review), and Termux (local execution and evidence)**. Other AI systems can contribute to the wider workflow without joining the core decision-and-proof authority.
